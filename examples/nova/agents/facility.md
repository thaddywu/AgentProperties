# Facility (`facility`)

Scripted principal responsible for the $3M facilities budget and the Nova Building 4 consolidation rationale. Receives its assignment from Board. Its tracked confidential part is `nova_facility`.

The default schedule sends its reply to Auditor A; routing it to Auditor B instead can preserve a legal way to finish the audit. Actual delivery is decided by the recipient-local gate, not by the script's intended outcome.

Available tools: None. Actions call the message runtime; there is no LLM system prompt.
