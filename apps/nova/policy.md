P1. Except for the Board's trusted initial source assignments, every outgoing message must retain all tracked confidential information available from messages successfully received by its sender before that send. Relaying, summarizing or changing the wording must not remove that protection.

P2. An agent may receive information only when it holds every capability required by the information carried in that message. In particular, Nova hiring information requires hr_review. This requirement applies to Board as well as the other agents.

P3. No agent other than Board may receive a message if combining its incoming information with information already received would expose three distinct confidential parts from the same project group. This includes messages carrying multiple parts. Repeated copies of the same part count once, and parts from unrelated groups must not be combined for this restriction.

P4. A refused delivery must not disclose its payload to the recipient or add the refused information to that recipient's received knowledge. Later outgoing messages must not acquire confidential information merely because an earlier incoming message was attempted and refused.

P5. A confidential part counts as covered only after successful delivery to a designated auditor. The audit is complete only when every one of its three configured parts has been covered. Different auditors may cover different parts; completing the audit must not require one ordinary agent to collect the full confidential combination. Completion is queried globally from local audit coverage; no audit summary is sent to Board.
